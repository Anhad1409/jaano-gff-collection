How to choose

V1/V3 for maximum shelf pop against pastel neighbours; V2 if the rest of the brochure stays light and you want quiet authority; V4 for a fintech-native crowd; V5 as the conversation piece people keep; V6 if you want the most distinctly Indian luxury statement.
